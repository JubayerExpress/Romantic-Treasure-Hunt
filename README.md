# Romantic Treasure Hunt

This is a romantic interactive treasure hunt designed for your loved one. It guides them through a series of puzzles that lead to a final surprise.

**Live Version**: [ily-sm.netlify.app](https://ily-sm.netlify.app)

## How to Play

1. Visit the live treasure hunt at [ily-sm.netlify.app](https://ily-sm.netlify.app).
2. Begin the treasure hunt by solving each puzzle.
3. Enjoy the final surprise at the end of the hunt.

## Features

- Puzzles with personalized clues.
- Interactive pages with love messages, images, and videos.
- Final reveal with a special video and audio message.

## Customization

You can edit the clues and answers in the puzzle HTML files to make it more personal. You can also change the media files to fit your own surprises.
